Dining & Markets

Back to top

Taniti offers a variety of dining options and local markets that reflect the island’s culture and flavors. Visitors can enjoy fresh seafood, local rice dishes, American-style meals, and Pan-Asian cuisine.

  • Approximately 10 restaurants across the island
  • Two supermarkets and two small grocery stores
  • One 24-hour convenience store
  • Many entertainment venues located in Merriton Landing
