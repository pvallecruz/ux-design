Getting to Taniti

Back to top

Taniti is accessible by air and by cruise. Choose the option that best matches your schedule, budget, and preferred travel style.

  • Air: Best for faster arrivals and shorter trips.
  • Cruise: Great for visitors combining multiple destinations.
  • Tip: Confirm arrival time before scheduling tours or lodging check-in.

Rentals

Back to top

Rentals give you maximum flexibility. Choose a car for comfort, a scooter for easy parking, or a bike for scenic exploring.

  • Car rentals: Best for families, groups, and longer distances.
  • Scooters: Ideal for couples and quick trips around town.
  • Bikes: Great for short scenic rides and beach areas. Note: Helmets are required by law when biking.

Public Transportation

Back to top

Public transportation is a budget-friendly way to explore Taniti. Routes connect key areas such as town centers, major lodging zones, and popular attractions.

  • Recommended for: solo travelers and visitors staying near major routes
  • Tip: Check schedules early and allow extra time during busy travel seasons
  • Accessibility: some routes may have limited accessibility options

Route Schedule

Route First Departure Last Departure Frequency
Downtown Loop 6:00 AM 11:00 PM Every 30 min
Beach Route 7:00 AM 10:30 PM Every 45 min
Resort Connector 6:30 AM 11:00 PM Every 60 min

Public buses operate daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. within Taniti City.

Taxis

Back to top

Taxis are a quick option for point-to-point trips. They work well for airport transfers, dinner plans, and travel when you don’t want to drive.

  • Recommended for: short trips, late-night returns, airport pickup
  • Tip: Confirm estimated fare before the ride when possible
  • Plan ahead during peak travel times
