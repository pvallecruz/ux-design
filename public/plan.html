Before You Go

Back to top

A little planning goes a long way. Use this checklist to arrive prepared and avoid last-minute stress.

  • Pack smart: light layers, comfortable shoes, sun protection, and bug spray.
  • Documents: keep IDs and confirmations accessible (digital + backup).
  • Plan activities: book popular tours early, especially during peak season.
  • Accessibility needs: confirm lodging and transport options in advance.

During Your Stay

Back to top

Once you arrive, focus on comfort, flexibility, and safety. These tips help you enjoy Taniti smoothly.

  • Transportation: taxis for quick trips, rentals for flexibility, transit for budget travel.
  • Stay hydrated: bring water for hikes and beach days.
  • Respect nature: follow trail guidance and posted safety signs.
  • Family-friendly planning: choose calmer beaches and earlier tour times.

Wrap up your trip smoothly with a quick checklist and a few ideas for what to bring home.
