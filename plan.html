Travel Essentials

Back to top

These quick facts help you plan confidently and avoid surprises during your trip.

  • Currency: U.S. dollars are used across the island, and credit cards are widely accepted.
  • Electricity: 120 volts (U.S.-style outlets).
  • Language: English is commonly spoken in Taniti City but may be limited in rural areas.
  • Medical Care: One hospital and multiple clinics serve the island.
  • Safety: Violent crime is rare, but normal precautions in crowded areas are recommended.
  • Alcohol Sales: Not permitted between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
  • Holidays: Some attractions and restaurants may close on local holidays.

Accessibility & Inclusion

Back to top

Taniti welcomes visitors of all abilities. Accessibility accommodations may vary by location, so advance planning is recommended.

  • Lodging: Some hotels and resorts offer accessible rooms and facilities.
  • Transportation: Public transit accessibility may vary by route.
  • Tours & Activities: Confirm mobility needs when booking guided experiences.
  • Medical Support: The island hospital and clinics provide multilingual assistance.

Before You Go

Back to top

A little planning goes a long way. Use this checklist to arrive prepared and avoid last-minute stress.

  • Pack smart: light layers, comfortable shoes, sun protection, and bug spray.
  • Documents: keep IDs and confirmations accessible (digital + backup).
  • Plan activities: book popular tours early, especially during peak season.
  • Arrival options: most visitors arrive by air; a cruise ship docks once per week.
  • Accessibility needs: confirm lodging and transportation options in advance.

During Your Stay

Back to top

Once you arrive, focus on comfort, flexibility, and safety. These tips help you enjoy Taniti smoothly.

  • Transportation: public buses operate in Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.; taxis and rentals are available island-wide.
  • Stay hydrated: bring water for hikes and beach days.
  • Respect nature: follow trail guidance and posted safety signs.
  • Family-friendly planning: choose calmer beaches and earlier tour times.
